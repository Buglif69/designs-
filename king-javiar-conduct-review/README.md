## 4. Legal flags (Queensland)

- **Cabin audio.** Invasion of Privacy Act 1971 (Qld) s43 makes it an offence to use a listening device to record a private conversation you are not a party to. A dashcam recording driver + passenger conversation is exposed to this. If audio is ON, treat it as a liability: no listening, no sharing, legal advice first, and consider switching audio off fleet-wide with written notice to staff.
- **Monitoring notice.** Qld has no workplace surveillance act, but a signed vehicle-use and camera-monitoring policy is what makes reliance on footage defensible. Check the HR file. If none exists, issue one now for the future and note the gap.
- **Fair process.** Under 15 staff: Small Business Fair Dismissal Code applies, but you still need a valid reason, written allegations, a chance to respond with a support person, and a decision made *after* the meeting. Put each allegation separately: (a) driving conduct / WHS, (b) unaccounted time and job not performed 05/09, (c) unauthorised passenger or conduct toward passenger (only if the facts support it), (d) service quality (keep separate).
- **Passenger.** Identify them and their role before drafting anything. If they have a complaint, their welfare and account come first (Sex Discrimination Act positive duty). Camera footage is supporting evidence, not the case.
- **Conflict of interest.** Same surname as the owner. Have an external HR adviser or independent manager conduct and minute the meeting.
