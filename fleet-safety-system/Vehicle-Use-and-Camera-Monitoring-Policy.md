# Slug A Bug Pest Solutions — Vehicle Use and In-Vehicle Camera Policy

**Applies to:** every employee and contractor who drives a company vehicle
**Issued:** ____ / ____ / 2026 **Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Jason Cartledge, Director

> **Why this exists.** We put cameras in the vehicles to protect our drivers in
> a crash, protect our customers, and keep our insurance and WHS obligations
> met. This policy tells you exactly what is recorded, who can see it, and what
> we expect behind the wheel. Sign it once and keep a copy.

---

## 1. The vehicle

1.1 Company vehicles are for company work. Reasonable direct travel between
home and your first and last job of the day is included.

1.2 **Personal use** outside that requires prior approval from the Director.
Approval is per-occasion unless you have written standing approval.

1.3 **Passengers.** No passenger in a company vehicle unless they are (a) a
Slug A Bug employee or contractor, (b) a person the Director has approved in
advance, or (c) a person you are assisting in an emergency. Customers,
friends, partners and family are not authorised without prior approval.

1.4 You must hold a current, unrestricted licence valid for the vehicle and
tell the Director within 24 hours of any suspension, disqualification,
demerit-point notice, or licence condition change.

1.5 **Fuel cards and cash floats** are for job-related expenditure only. Every
non-customer stop made on work time must be logged on the job card with a
reason.

1.6 Report any damage, warning light, or roadworthiness concern before the
next use. Do not drive a vehicle you believe is unsafe.

## 2. Driving standards

2.1 Obey every road rule, speed limit and site rule, without exception. A
running late is never a reason. If you are late, call the customer.

2.2 **Zero tolerance:** drink or drug driving, driving while suspended, using
a handheld phone while moving, street racing, deliberate aggressive or
retaliatory driving, or leaving the scene of an incident. Any of these is
serious misconduct and may end your employment immediately.

2.3 We monitor harsh-driving events (harsh braking, acceleration, cornering,
impact). Repeated events are handled by coaching first — see the Harsh Driving
Escalation SOP. It only becomes a disciplinary matter if it keeps happening
after coaching, or if a single event is severe.

2.4 Fatigue is a hazard. If you are not fit to drive, say so. You will not be
penalised for calling it.

2.5 **Fines and infringements** are the driver's responsibility and will be
nominated to you.

## 3. The camera — what is recorded

3.1 Every company vehicle carries a forward-facing (and where fitted,
interior-facing) camera that records:

- **Continuously to an SD card** while the vehicle is running, overwriting
  oldest-first
- **To the cloud** when a trigger fires: harsh braking, harsh acceleration,
  harsh cornering, impact while driving, impact while parked
- **GPS position and speed**, and trip start/end points

3.2 **Cabin audio: ☐ ENABLED ☐ DISABLED** *(tick one at issue — see note below)*

> If audio is enabled, it will be stated here in writing before it is turned
> on, and every driver must sign acknowledging it. Recording a private
> conversation you are not a party to can breach the *Invasion of Privacy Act
> 1971* (Qld) s43. Where audio is not required for safety, it stays off.

3.3 Recording runs whenever the vehicle is operating. It is not switched on or
off in response to any individual driver, and it is not aimed at monitoring
your conversations or your private life.

3.4 **Do not** disable, obstruct, unplug, reposition, or remove the camera, or
remove or wipe the SD card, without the Director's instruction. Doing so is
treated as a serious matter in its own right, separate from whatever was
recorded.

## 4. Who can see the footage, and when

4.1 Footage is accessed only by the Director or a person the Director
authorises, and only for:

- investigating a crash, near miss, damage or injury
- responding to a customer or public complaint
- an insurance or legal claim
- driver safety coaching
- a workplace investigation where there is a genuine reason to look

4.2 Footage is **not** used for routine surveillance of how you spend your day,
and is not reviewed at random.

4.3 Footage relied on in any disciplinary process will be shown to you, and you
will be given a fair chance to respond before any decision is made.

4.4 Footage is retained only as long as needed for the purpose above, then
deleted, except where it must be kept for a claim, investigation or legal
requirement.

4.5 Personal information in footage is handled under the Privacy Act 1988
(Cth) and our privacy obligations. You may ask what footage of you is held.

## 5. If you have a crash or incident

1. Stop. Check for injuries. Call 000 if anyone is hurt.
2. Do not admit liability.
3. Exchange details. Photograph the scene.
4. Call the Director the same day — before you continue working.
5. Do not delete or overwrite anything on the camera.

## 6. If something goes wrong at work

If you are being treated badly by a colleague, a customer, or anyone else you
meet on the job — including anything sexual, threatening or discriminatory —
tell the Director or Ashleigh straight away. We are required to act on it, and
you will not be punished for raising it.

## 7. Breach

Breaching this policy may lead to coaching, a written warning, loss of vehicle
access, or termination, depending on how serious it is and what has happened
before. Serious misconduct (clause 2.2, or tampering under 3.4) may result in
immediate dismissal. You will be told what the concern is and given a chance to
respond before any decision.
